# Pardus 25 GNOME desktop

Facts checked on Pardus 25.2 GNOME (GNOME 48, Wayland). The interface language is
Turkish. Positions change with screen size, so always confirm on the screenshot.

## Layout
- One panel along the bottom of the screen (Dash to Panel), no top bar.
- Bottom-left corner: the Pardus logo button, tooltip "Uygulamaları Göster" (Show Apps). It opens the full-screen app grid.
- Next to it, pinned apps: Firefox, Dosyalar (Files, blue icon), Pardus Yazılım Merkezi (Software Center, dark round icon). Running apps also appear here.
- Bottom-right: settings gear, clipboard icon, the clock and date (click for the calendar and notifications), and network, volume and power icons (click any of them for the quick settings menu: Wi-Fi, sound, dark style, power off).
- Desktop icons on the left: "Ev" (Home folder), "Çöp" (Trash), "Bilgisayar" (Computer). They open with a double-click.
- Windows have minimise, maximise and close buttons at the top-right of their title bar.

## App grid ("Uygulamaları Göster")
- A search field at the top: "Yazarak ara" (type to search). Typing right after opening the grid searches apps and settings.
- Apps open with a single click. The grid has more than one page (dots at the bottom, arrow on the right).
- Some apps are grouped in folders: "Araçlar" (Tools), "Sistem" (System).
- Useful names: Ayarlar (Settings), Dosyalar (Files), Konsol (terminal), Hesap Makinesi (Calculator), Ekran Görüntüsü (Screenshot), Saatler (Clocks), Hava Durumu (Weather), Çizim (Drawing), Belge Tarayıcı (Document Scanner), Yardım (Help).
- Escape or a click on an empty area closes the grid.

## Desktop right-click menu
Right-click an empty part of the desktop. Items, top to bottom:
"Yeni Klasör" (New Folder), "Yeni Belge" (New Document, submenu), "Yapıştır" (Paste),
"Tümünü Seç" (Select All), "Simgeleri Sırala" (Arrange Icons), "Sırala..." (Arrange By, submenu),
"Masaüstünü Dosyalar'da Göster" (Show Desktop in Files), "Uçbirimde Aç" (Open in Terminal),
"Arka Planı Değiştir..." (Change Background), "Masaüstü Simgeleri Ayarları" (Desktop Icons Settings),
"Görüntü Ayarları" (Display Settings).
Never use "Uçbirimde Aç": Clicky does not use the terminal.

## Common tasks
- Change the wallpaper: right-click the desktop, choose "Arka Planı Değiştir...". Settings opens on its appearance page; click one of the wallpaper thumbnails. It applies at once.
- Dark or light style: same appearance page in Ayarlar, or the quick settings menu at the bottom-right.
- New folder on the desktop: right-click the desktop, "Yeni Klasör", type the name, press Return.
- Open a folder: double-click "Ev" on the desktop, or open Dosyalar from the panel.
- Organise files: in Dosyalar, create folders with right-click > "Yeni Klasör", then drag files onto a folder to move them.
- Screen resolution and scaling: right-click the desktop, "Görüntü Ayarları".
- Open an app: Pardus button, then click the app (or type its name in "Yazarak ara").
